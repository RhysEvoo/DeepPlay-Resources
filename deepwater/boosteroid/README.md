# Boosteroid

Sign in to Boosteroid with a QR code on your phone, then browse and search the games in your Boosteroid library. Play them on the PS3 (Project DeepWater 00.72 and on): Start + Select leaves a game, L3 + R3 is the Xbox button. Not made by or affiliated with Boosteroid. Built on what the open-source ZERODROID client worked out.

| --- | --- |

Install it on the PS3 from DeepHub, DeepPlay's app store. These files are made by `publish-plugin.ps1` in the DeepPlay source.
