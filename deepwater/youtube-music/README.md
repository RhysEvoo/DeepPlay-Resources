# YouTube Music

Browse YouTube Music's home, new releases and charts, search for songs, albums, artists and playlists, and play them on your PS3. Music keeps playing in the background while you go round DeepPlay, with the Control Center's Music card to pause and skip, and stops when a game starts. Save songs, albums and playlists to your library on this PS3. No sign-in needed. Not made by or affiliated with Google or YouTube.
