# CloudMoon

Play the mobile games CloudMoon streams from cloud Android phones (Roblox, Genshin Impact and more) on your PS3: as a guest with CloudMoon's free play time, or signed in with your own CloudMoon account from your phone. The controller plays each game on its own layout (L3 + R3 for a pointer), and a USB keyboard and mouse play as on CloudMoon's website. Not made by or affiliated with CloudMoon.
