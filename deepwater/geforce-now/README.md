# GeForce NOW

Find any of the games GeForce NOW streams, add them to your library, and play them on your PS3. Sign in with your NVIDIA account, or your ABYA or other partner account, and link Steam, Epic and other stores from the Account tab. Not made by or affiliated with NVIDIA.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Version | 0.14 |
| Publisher | DeepPlay |
| Needs | A GeForce NOW account (NVIDIA, or a partner such as ABYA) |
| Kind | Streaming, Cloud |

Install it on the PS3 from DeepPlay Plugins. These files are made by `publish-plugin.ps1` in the DeepPlay source.
